What the pictures are.

The official mark is a vertical blade of white-violet light on deep purple, with the wordmark FLINTTECH in wide-tracked caps underneath. That image is the company. Use it on the origin site, decks, profiles, and letterheads.

The TradeFlock magazine cover that pairs Bradley Dickover with that mark is a mockup. It is watermarked “Mockup Cover Story. Not to be published.” It is not a printed issue, not an award, and not a credential. Do not post it as a cover story. Do not say “Most Innovative Tech Leaders From USA 2026” as if it was conferred.

Lines that may be used

These lines appear on the mockup and are usable as founder voice. They are not magazine quotes.

  • AI. Autonomous systems. Real-world impact.
  • Autonomous engineering for a brighter tomorrow.
  • Turning complexity into a smarter, more autonomous tomorrow.
  • Innovation happens when people, purpose and technology move together.

Do not add “leadership at the intersection of defense and AI” unless a public, checkable defense program exists. Architecture first. Settlement when measured.

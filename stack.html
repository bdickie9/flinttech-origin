Design concept

One company. Six organs. Zero invented numbers.

The stack is not a slide. It is the way Flint stays a company when software wants to become theater.

Map

Perception binds to money. Policy binds to the founder.

Echo

Perception and memory. Health, heartbeat, voice, ledger. Lives with MIDAS rather than as a second cloud.

Meridian

Measured axis. Publishes only what endpoints return. The anti-hallucination layer for the company.

Flint

The operating company: brand, catalog, demand, fulfillment responsibility.

MIDAS

Money path. Checkout, webhook, storefront. Production API is the source of truth.

Helix

Revenue loop: demand, sale, settlement, lesson. No loop is closed on a simulated checkout.

Sovereign OS

Written law for a solo founder. Human leftovers stay listed, not wished away.

Forward-thinking rule

Autonomy without a counterfeit scoreboard.

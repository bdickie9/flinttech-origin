Biography

Build the company that can keep itself honest.

Bradley Scott Dickover is the founder and CEO of FlintTech Global, operating through Dickover Inc. / d|o. He works from Minnesota. His public engineering identity is github.com/bdickie9.

His contribution is architectural. He treats a solo-founded company as a production system: perception (Echo), measured state (Meridian), policy (Sovereign OS), money (MIDAS), and a demand-to-settlement loop (Helix). Specialist agents do scoped work. The founder remains accountable for live secrets, first commercial proof, and anything that cannot be automated without lying.

He will not perform inflated headcount, borrowed prestige, sentience theater, or a scoreboard that cannot be replayed from an endpoint. The public stance is practical sentientism: do not declare current models sentient; do give systems with memory, tools, and money access a constitution, a ledger, and an owner.

I am trying to build a company that can act like it has a nervous system without pretending it already has a soul.

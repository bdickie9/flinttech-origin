Why this product first

A real local market before a mythology.

Flint’s first public commercial object is not a general chatbot. It is a bounded seat in a bounded geography. That constraint is deliberate. It forces the company to price, fulfill, and settle something a working tradesperson can evaluate.

  • Market: Minneapolis and Saint Paul only
  • Model: one seat per trade per city
  • Rail: MIDAS checkout and Stripe webhook
  • Rule: paid sessions move only on verified paid events

Price is published on the live /buy page. This narrative page does not freeze a dollar figure that the catalog may correct.
